# 01 — Project Vision

## Project Status

Working project name: Business Development Site

Final brand name: TBD

The final brand name will be selected only after target audiences, services and positioning are approved. The missing brand name must not block the site structure or brand positioning documents. Domain, logo, metadata and final SEO naming will be addressed later.

Founder: Luca Pistonesi

Languages at launch:
- Italian
- English

The final website will represent an independent international Business Development brand.

It must NOT be designed as a personal CV website.

Luca Pistonesi is the Founder of the brand and his professional background provides credibility, experience and human representation to the organisation.

The brand is Founder-led, and the website must make this clear.

---

# 1. Vision

Create an independent Business Development brand focused on connecting:

- companies;
- entrepreneurs;
- products;
- markets;
- publishers;
- authors;
- commercial partners;
- distributors;
- professional expertise;
- international opportunities.

The brand exists to identify promising opportunities and help transform them into concrete business relationships.

Its role is not limited to traditional consultancy.

The brand should actively support the creation of commercial connections, market-entry opportunities, strategic partnerships and selected business initiatives.

The fundamental concept is:

> Connecting businesses, markets and opportunities.

The organisation acts as a bridge between people or companies that have complementary interests but have not yet found each other.

---

# 2. Core Professional Positioning

The brand operates primarily in:

## Business Development

Identification and development of new commercial opportunities.

Business Development may include:

- identification of new commercial opportunities;
- evaluation of selected products, services and projects with commercial potential;
- development of new business relationships;
- support in turning an opportunity into a concrete commercial conversation.

## Market Entry

Support for companies interested in approaching or entering the Italian market.

In selected cases, support may also be provided to Italian companies seeking international opportunities.

## Partner Search

Identification of potential:

- commercial partners;
- distributors;
- importers;
- publishers;
- licensees;
- professional partners;
- strategic counterparties.

## Strategic Partnerships

Development of collaborations between companies, professionals and organisations whose activities may create value together.

## Publishing & Rights Development

Author & Publishing Opportunities.

Development of opportunities related to:

- authors;
- publishers;
- books;
- publishing rights;
- international rights;
- licensing;
- editorial partnerships;
- special publishing projects.

## Public Service Naming

For the initial public positioning, the website must avoid language that could unnecessarily imply regulated agency or representation roles.

"Author Representation" must NOT be presented as a core public service at this stage.

Preferred public service names:

- Publishing & Rights Development
- Author & Publishing Opportunities
- Business Development
- Market Entry
- Partner Search
- Strategic Partnerships

# 3. What the Brand Is

The brand should be perceived as:

- independent;
- international;
- selective;
- credible;
- pragmatic;
- relationship-oriented;
- opportunity-driven;
- commercially aware;
- entrepreneurial.

It should communicate the ability to understand an opportunity, identify the appropriate people and create the conditions for a business conversation to begin.

The central value is not simply information.

The central value is:

> identifying the right opportunity and bringing the right parties together.

---

# 4. What the Brand Is Not

The website must NOT present the organisation as:

- a generic consulting firm;
- a marketing agency;
- a traditional sales agency;
- a recruitment company;
- an investment bank;
- a law firm;
- a financial advisor;
- a large multinational consultancy;
- an expert in every industry.

The organisation must never claim capabilities, offices, employees, partnerships, customers or international presence that do not actually exist.

The brand should appear ambitious but credible.

---

# 5. Founder Role

Luca Pistonesi is the Founder.

His role is primarily related to:

- identifying opportunities;
- developing new projects;
- initiating relationships;
- evaluating potential partnerships;
- connecting suitable parties;
- coordinating selected initiatives;
- supporting early commercial discussions;
- developing business concepts;
- supervising projects and external specialists when required.

The Founder should not be presented as the technical expert in every sector.

When a project requires specific expertise, independent professionals or sector specialists may be involved on a case-by-case basis.

The website must NOT imply that an established partner network already exists.

The Founder therefore acts primarily as:

- entrepreneur;
- business developer;

- relationship builder;
- opportunity developer.

# 10. Operating Philosophy

The working process should generally follow this logic:

Opportunity
→ Evaluation
→ Identification of relevant parties
→ Introduction
→ Business conversation
→ Development
→ Agreement or next step

Not every opportunity should be accepted.

Selection is part of the brand’s positioning.

The brand should prioritise opportunities where it can realistically contribute value.

# 12. Revenue Model

- advisory fee;

- retainer;
- licensing participation;
- revenue share;

# 13. Website Purpose

The website has five primary objectives.

## 1. Explain the Brand

A visitor must quickly understand:

- what the organisation does;
- what type of opportunities it develops;
- who it works with.

## 2. Generate Qualified Contacts

The website should encourage companies and professionals to submit meaningful opportunities.

Version 1 will include a structured professional contact form.

This is NOT an opportunity marketplace or a public opportunity-submission platform.

The form may classify enquiries by type, aligned with the service architecture defined in document 02:

- Business Development;
- Market Entry;
- Partner Search;
- Strategic Partnerships;
- Publishing & Rights;
- Author & Publishing Opportunities;
- Other.

"Publishing & Rights" is the shorter public / contact-form label. The formal service name remains "Publishing & Rights Development".

International context is captured through country, target market and the opportunity description, rather than through a separate "International Opportunity" category.

## 3. Build Credibility

The Founder’s professional history and selected past activities should demonstrate real-world experience.

Version 1 will not contain formal case studies.

## 4. Present Services

The website must clearly describe the organisation’s core areas of activity.

## 5. Support International Development

The English website must be designed for international visitors, particularly organisations interested in the Italian market.

# 15. Brand Personality

The brand must communicate:

- discretion;
- competence;
- curiosity;
- selectivity;
- international orientation;
- reliability;
- entrepreneurial thinking;
- clarity.

The tone must be confident without being arrogant.

## Brand Voice

The website may use "we" as the voice of the independent brand.

"We" must never imply employees, offices, a permanent team or resources that do not exist.

The website must make clear that the brand is Founder-led by Luca Pistonesi.

Avoid language such as:

- "We guarantee success"
- "Global leader"
- "World-class"
- "Best in the market"
- "Unmatched expertise"
- "We can sell anything"

unless such statements can be objectively demonstrated.

Prefer language such as:

- "We develop selected opportunities."
- "We connect companies with relevant partners."
- "We support market-entry conversations."
- "We identify potential commercial relationships."
- "We evaluate projects case by case."

---

# 16. Visual Direction

The visual identity should feel:

- international;
- sophisticated;
- contemporary;
- minimal;
- professional;
- editorial;
- premium but understated.

The website should NOT resemble:

- a flashy marketing agency;
- a crypto startup;
- a corporate template;
- a social-media influencer website.

The visitor should perceive discretion, quality and professional seriousness.

---

# 17. Bilingual Strategy

Launch languages:

- Italian
- English

Italian and English are both official launch languages.

The brand is international.

English will serve as the principal international-facing language.

Italian will be a complete equivalent version.

URL architecture, redirects and technical localisation are not decided at this stage.

The English version must NOT be a mechanical word-for-word translation.

It should be professionally localised for an international business audience.

Both language versions should communicate the same positioning while adapting wording where necessary.
